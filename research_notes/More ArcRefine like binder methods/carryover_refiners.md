# Structural-carryover / template-conditioned / pose-preserving binder refinement methods BEYOND the obvious (2024–2026)

> Scope: the family ArcRefine (FoldArc) belongs to — methods that carry a reference/validated binding geometry (template, motif, substructure, embedding, or the complex itself) through an iterative refinement loop to mature sequence/affinity/expression while PRESERVING the docked pose. This file deliberately excludes what the sibling note "ArcRefine like binder methods/" already cataloged (ArcRefine, Mosaic, BindCraft, BoltzDesign1, PXDesign-h, ColabDesign/AfDesign, EvoBind2, Germinal, dl_binder_design/AF2-IG) and focuses on the methods we likely MISSED: diffusion/flow structural carryover, co-folder template conditioning, and pose-preserving ΔΔG / directed-evolution affinity maturation. Target application = a small (60–120 aa, 0-Cys) de-novo human-TNFα pH-switch mini-binder. Research date 2026-10-08.
>
> **ArcRefine's mechanism (the anchor), for relating everything below:** a thin Mosaic fork that seeds the optimizer from an existing design + bound-target complex and **carries Boltz-2's single & pair representations across sequence-update steps** (initialized from the starting-complex prediction), so every gradient update is biased toward the already-found binding mode; final sequence is re-predicted with no carried state as an honesty check. It is *embedding-side, optimization-time* carryover.

## KQ1: Is there a published "ArcRefine"/"FoldArc" paper? Who are its direct analogues/competitors?

### Takeaway
No peer-reviewed ArcRefine/FoldArc paper exists — only a single-author Zenodo preprint (Ken Osumi, FoldArc Co., 2026-10-05), with no arXiv/PubMed index (confirmed again here; my searches surfaced nothing new). Its true analogues split into three mechanism classes the sibling note under-covered: (a) **diffusion/flow "carry a motif/substructure/backbone" generators** (partial diffusion, motif-scaffolding flow models, inpainting), (b) **co-folder template/restraint conditioning** (Boltz-2, Chai-1, AF3, Protenix), and (c) **pose-preserving ΔΔG / directed-evolution affinity maturation** (EvoPro, GearBind, RDE-PPI, ThermoMPNN, FEP, plus 2026 flow-matching maturation like PPIFlow/AffinityFlow).

### Cited Findings
- No arXiv/bioRxiv/PubMed record for "ArcRefine" or "FoldArc structural carryover" was returned by any query; the only artifacts remain GitHub `ken-osumi/ArcRefine`, Zenodo (concept DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23115832), and foldarc.com — consistent with the sibling note's provenance finding (unverified single-author preprint). — [Zenodo record](https://zenodo.org/records/23115832)
- The closest *named* competitor by mechanism (embedding/structure-as-energy for binder design) is **BindEnergyCraft: Casting Protein Structure Predictors as Energy-Based Models for Binder Design** — [arXiv 2505.21241 (2025)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2505.21241).
- The closest *task* competitor (optimize/diversify an existing designed binder in silico) with a 2026 preprint is **"High-Affinity Protein Binder Design via Flow Matching and In Silico Maturation" (PPIFlow)** — [bioRxiv 10.64898/2026.01.19.700484](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.01.19.700484.full.pdf) (note the unusual `10.64898` DOI prefix; verify) — and **"Mapping the evolution of computationally designed protein binders"** (continuous hypermutation + beam search over ProteinMPNN/AF-Multimer with iPAE reward) — [bioRxiv 2025.10.04.680454](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.10.04.680454.full.pdf).

### Inferences
- ArcRefine is best understood as *one point* in a broad carryover design space. Its specific novelty (cross-step Boltz-2 embedding reuse) appears genuinely unpackaged elsewhere, but its *goal* — refine a validated pose, change most residues, keep the interface — is served by many open methods below, several license-cleaner than ArcRefine's PolyForm Noncommercial.

### Gaps
- No independent (non-FoldArc) validation of the carryover trick exists. Whether cross-step embedding reuse beats a plain template/partial-diffusion carryover has not been benchmarked by anyone.

## KQ2: Methods that explicitly carry a template / reference structure through refinement

### Takeaway
Three sub-families carry structure explicitly: (A) **diffusion/flow generators that fix a motif/substructure/backbone and regenerate the rest** — partial diffusion (RFdiffusion, RFdiffusion3), motif-scaffolding flow models (FrameFlow, FoldFlow-2, Genie2), substructure conditioning (Chroma), and framework-preserving RFantibody; (B) **co-folder template + restraint conditioning** (Boltz-2 multi-chain templates + distance/pocket constraints + Feynman-Kac steering; Chai-1 templates + contact/pocket restraints; AF3/Protenix templates) — the prediction-side analog of ArcRefine's optimization-side carryover; and (C) a new generator **BoltzGen** with a `protein-redesign` partial-redesign mode. "GRADE" as a named method could not be found — see Gaps.

### Cited Findings

**(A) Diffusion / flow structural carryover (fix the interface motif, regenerate the scaffold)**
- **RFdiffusion3 (RFD3)** — all-atom diffusion generating explicit side chains + backbone while reasoning about non-protein context; **partial diffusion is a built-in task** (start denoising from a chosen timestep on an existing structure rather than from noise) → the direct structure-side carryover of a validated binder. ~10× faster than RFd2; matched/beat prior tools on protein–protein/DNA/small-molecule/enzyme benchmarks. **Open-sourced Dec 2025**, training code + weights via Rosetta Commons Foundry; related RosettaCommons tools (RFd2/RFd3/MPNN) are **BSD-3-Clause**. — [De novo Design of All-atom Biomolecular Interactions with RFdiffusion3, PMC12458353](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12458353/); [IPD "RFdiffusion3 now available" (2025-12)](https://www.ipd.uw.edu/2025/12/rfdiffusion3-now-available/); [GEN coverage](https://www.genengnews.com/topics/artificial-intelligence/rfdiffusion3-now-open-source-designs-dna-binders-and-advanced-enzymes)
- **FrameFlow — "Improved motif-scaffolding with SE(3) flow matching"** — SE(3) flow-matching backbone model extended to scaffold a FIXED motif two ways: **motif amortization** (train with motif as input via data augmentation) and **motif guidance** (estimate the conditional score at inference, no retraining). 2.5× more designable+unique scaffolds vs prior SOTA on 24 motifs. — [bioRxiv/PMC10802670](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC10802670/); [arXiv 2401.04082 (2024-01)](https://arxiv.org/html/2401.04082v2)
- **FoldFlow-2** — SE(3) flow-matching structure generator; supports **motif scaffolding**, beats RFdiffusion on unconditional designability/diversity/novelty, and makes progress on hard conditional tasks incl. **VHH-nanobody scaffold design**. — [arXiv 2405.20313 (2024-05)](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2405.20313)
- **Genie 2** — diffusion backbone model with a **multi-motif scaffolding** framework; motif conditioning is **SE(3)-invariant** (encodes motif residues as one-hot + motif Cα pairwise-distance matrix) so it does NOT fix absolute motif coordinates — unlike RFdiffusion/FrameFlow which fix motif coordinates and are sensitive to initial placement. Solves more motif-scaffolding problems than prior methods; SOTA designability/diversity/novelty. — [arXiv 2405.15489 (2024-05)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2405.15489)
- **Chroma** — programmable generative model framing design as **Bayesian inference under constraints**, with explicit conditioners for **substructure, symmetry, shape, semantics, and NN-predicted classes**. The *substructure conditioner* carries a reference sub-structure (e.g., the interface) through sampling. Experimentally validated (310 proteins; 2 crystal structures). Code `github.com/generatebio/chroma` (code Apache-2.0; weights require accepting Generate's license — verify). — [Nature 2023, 623:1070–1078, 10.1038/s41586-023-06728-8](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37968394/); [bioRxiv 2022.12.01.518682](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2022.12.01.518682v1.full.pdf); [repo](https://github.com/generatebio/chroma)
- **RFantibody** — RFdiffusion **fine-tuned so the antibody/VHH framework stays FIXED while new CDR loops are generated around a defined epitope** (framework = a carried structural template); pipeline = Ab-finetuned RFdiffusion → ProteinMPNN → Ab-finetuned RoseTTAFold2 filter. Code `github.com/RosettaCommons/RFantibody` (license not explicitly confirmed; one mirror claims MIT, sibling RosettaCommons tools are BSD-3 — check LICENSE). — [Bennett et al, Nature 2025 (s41586-025-09721-5)](https://www.ipd.uw.edu/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Bennett-Nature-2025-RFantibody-s41586-025-09721-5.pdf); [bioRxiv 2024.03.14.585103](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2024.03.14.585103.full.pdf); [repo](https://github.com/RosettaCommons/RFantibody)
- **Proteina / "Scaling Atomistic Protein Binder Design with Generative Pretraining and Test-Time Compute"** — large flow-based atomistic generator with motif/scaffold conditioning and **test-time-compute** scaling of binder design (the sibling note's "Proteina-Complexa" line). — [Proteina, arXiv 2503.00710 (2025-03)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2503.00710); [Scaling Atomistic…, arXiv 2603.27950 (ICLR 2026)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.27950)

**(B) Co-folder template + restraint conditioning (prediction-side carryover)**
- **Boltz-2** — controllability features: **multi-chain template integration, distance/pocket constraints, experimental-method conditioning**, and **Feynman-Kac steering inference-time potentials** for physically valid poses; also a **binding-affinity head approaching FEP accuracy**. Feeding the bound complex as a template/restraint pins the pose during refold — the same stack ArcRefine runs on. MIT. — [Boltz-2 preprint 10.1101/2025.06.14.659707](https://prereview.org/preprints/doi-10.1101-2025.06.14.659707/write-a-prereview); [aiwiki Boltz-2](https://www.aiwiki.ai/wiki/boltz_2/raw)
- **Chai-1** — all-atom co-folder with **template loading** (m8 hit table → RCSB or local `CHAI_TEMPLATE_CIF_FOLDER`) and **contact/pocket restraints** via CSV (inter-chain residue–residue contacts at several resolutions); conditioning on **just 4 epitope residues more than doubled prediction success**. Code `github.com/chaidiscovery/chai-lab` (Apache-2.0 code; weights have a community/commercial-use caveat — verify). — [Chai-1 docs/overview](https://www.benchling.com/ai/models/chai-1); [Chai-1 restraints how-to](https://blog.samson-connect.net/guided-structure-predictions-with-chai-1-using-pocket-and-contact-restraints)
- **AF3 / Protenix / OpenFold3** — all accept templates; Protenix is driveable by Mosaic (so an indirect template-carryover path on the user's stack). (Template-conditioning is standard AF3-lineage behavior; treat as the generic prediction-side carryover primitive.) — inferred from [Mosaic backends](https://github.com/escalante-bio/mosaic) (sibling note) + Boltz/Chai template features above.

**(C) New generator with an explicit partial-redesign mode**
- **BoltzGen ("Toward Universal Binder Design")** — unified all-atom diffusion binder generator (protein/peptide/nanobody vs protein/peptide/small-molecule): diffusion backbone → inverse folding (default **SolubleMPNN**) → refold (ESMFold2/Boltz2) → filter (PLIP H-bonds, SASA, RMSD, Boltz2 affinity). Has a **`protein-redesign` protocol with `design_mask`/`design` to mark residue regions for redesign** (scaffold/partial conditioning) and a dedicated **`boltzgen1_adherence`** checkpoint, though docs emphasize de-novo generation over pose refinement. **MIT** (per release notes); `pip install boltzgen` (py≥3.11), weights auto-download from HF (`boltzgen/*`, ~6 GB + ~33 GB ESMFold2). — [github.com/HannesStark/boltzgen README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/HannesStark/boltzgen/main/README.md); [MIT jclinic "Introducing BoltzGen"](https://jclinic.mit.edu/boltzgen/)

### Inferences
- **Diffusion/flow carryover is the structure-side mirror of ArcRefine.** ArcRefine keeps the pose via *embeddings* during gradient updates; partial diffusion (RFd3), motif-scaffolding (FrameFlow/FoldFlow2/Genie2/Chroma), and RFantibody keep it via a *fixed motif/substructure/backbone* during generation. For the TNFα binder, the highest-leverage use is: **hold the TNF-contacting residues fixed and regenerate the rest of the 60–120 aa scaffold** → a fold-diversifying novelty lever that preserves the validated interface (same selling point ArcRefine claims with its "2/3 residues changed").
- **Co-folder template conditioning is the cheapest carryover the user can add today** — Boltz-2/Chai-1 template + contact restraints pin the pH-7.4 bound pose at refold/validation time (a stronger version of the AF2-initial-guess the user already runs), with no new training.
- Genie2's SE(3)-invariant (coordinate-free) motif conditioning is attractive when the exact rigid placement of the TNF interface motif is uncertain; RFd3/FrameFlow fix coordinates and need a good initial placement.

### Gaps
- **"GRADE" could not be identified as a named binder-design/refinement method** across three targeted searches. Closest guided-diffusion analogs are **NOS (diffusioN Optimized Sampling, guided discrete diffusion, Gruver et al, [arXiv 2305.20009](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.20009), NeurIPS 2023)** and **Constrained Diffusion for protein design ([arXiv 2510.14989](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.14989v1), 2025)**. If "GRADE" is real it is likely very recent, private/vendor, or a renamed tool — flag for the user to supply a link.
- Did not read Chai-1 / Chroma LICENSE files byte-level; code licenses stated above are from secondary sources and need a direct check before non-research use.
- "Protenix/AF3 template conditioning for *design* (not just prediction)" was inferred, not confirmed from a primary design-use source this round.

## KQ3: Gradient / structure-guided refinement loops that preserve a pose while maturing affinity

### Takeaway
Two mature classes here, both largely absent from the sibling note: (1) **ΔΔG mutation-effect predictors that mutate the complex in place** (pose preserved by construction — RDE-PPI, GearBind, ThermoMPNN, HERMES, DiffAffinity, FoldX/Rosetta Flex-ddG, FEP) — the purest "pose-preserving affinity maturation," ideal as a downstream ranker on a fixed TNFα complex; and (2) **directed-evolution / flow loops with an explicit pose-restraint term** (EvoPro's "conformational stability" penalty; PPIFlow's fix-interface-residues-then-noise-then-flow-refine; AffinityFlow's alternating structure-guide↔inverse-fold). These relate to ArcRefine as the non-gradient (GA) and flow-matching counterparts of its carryover loop.

### Cited Findings

**(1) Pose-preserving ΔΔG maturation (mutate the complex in place — pose cannot drift)**
- **RDE-PPI (Rotamer Density Estimator)** — unsupervised learner of the **effect of mutations on protein–protein binding ΔΔG** via rotamer density; operates on the bound complex. Code `github.com/luost26/RDE-PPI` (weights + scripts included). ICLR 2023. Directly ranks affinity-improving point mutations on a fixed TNFα interface. — [repo](https://github.com/luost26/RDE-PPI); [ar5iv (DiffAffinity companion)](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2310.19849)
- **GearBind** — pretrainable geometric GNN (GearNet backbone, multi-relational/multi-level message passing, contrastive structural pretraining) for **in-silico affinity maturation**; beats SOTA on SKEMPI; raised CR3022–Omicron affinity up to **17×** and a human VHH (UdAb) vs 5T4 up to **5.6×**. Nat Commun 2024. — [Nat Commun 2024 (s41467-024-51563-8)](https://ideas.repec.org/a/nat/natcom/v15y2024i1d10.1038_s41467-024-51563-8.html); [bioRxiv 2023.08.10.552845](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2023.08.10.552845.full.pdf)
- **ThermoMPNN** — ProteinMPNN-embedding → ΔΔG head for per-mutation **stability** (and a binding variant); fast single-mutant scans that keep the structure fixed. Code `github.com/Kuhlman-Lab/ThermoMPNN` (PNAS 2024). Good for the *expression/stability* axis (Adaptyv's hard gate) without moving the pose. — [repo via Kuhlman-Lab](https://docs.tamarind.bio/tools/thermompnn)
- **HERMES** — holographic equivariant NN for **mutational effect + stability** prediction (3D-structure-conditioned). — [arXiv 2407.06703 (2024)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2407.06703)
- **DiffAffinity** — predicts mutational effects on PPI binding via a **side-chain diffusion probabilistic model** (NeurIPS 2023). — [ar5iv 2310.19849](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2310.19849)
- **"Predicting mutational effects on protein binding from folding energy"** (2025) — binding-ΔΔG from folding-energy models; a recent entrant on the SKEMPI-style task. — [arXiv 2507.05502 (2025)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2507.05502)
- **Physics baselines (pose carried by construction):** FoldX and **Rosetta Flex-ddG** (ddG scanning on the fixed complex) and **FEP/alchemical** methods remain the classical pose-preserving maturation tools; **Boltz-2's affinity head "approaches FEP accuracy"** so Boltz-2 mutation-scanning on the held complex is a learned, GPU-cheap stand-in. — [aiwiki Boltz-2 (FEP-level affinity)](https://www.aiwiki.ai/wiki/boltz_2/raw) (FoldX/Flex-ddG/FEP are standard; no primary fetch this round — see Gaps)

**(2) Directed-evolution / flow loops with an explicit pose restraint**
- **EvoPro (Kuhlman Lab)** — genetic algorithm = iterative AF2 structure prediction + sequence diversification (ProteinMPNN or random mutagenesis); multi-objective score includes **Placement Confidence (iPAE), Fold Confidence (pLDDT), and "Conformational Stability" — explicitly minimizing the structural change required for binding** (i.e., a pose-drift penalty). Reaches pM–nM affinities in silico. Code `github.com/Kuhlman-Lab/evopro`. This is the GA analog of ArcRefine: carryover enforced by a loss, not embeddings. — [repo](https://github.com/Kuhlman-Lab/evopro); [Tamarind EvoPro](https://www.tamarind.bio/tools/evopro)
- **PPIFlow — "High-Affinity Protein Binder Design via Flow Matching and In Silico Maturation"** (2026-01) — flow-matching backbone → ProteinMPNN/AbMPNN → AF3Score filter → **in-silico maturation = interface rotamer enrichment, select/merge key interface residues, FIX critical residues + add noise + flow-based refinement of the unconstrained regions** = partial-noising carryover of the interface while maturing the rest. De-novo VEGFA binder at single-digit nM. — [bioRxiv 10.64898/2026.01.19.700484](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.01.19.700484.full.pdf)
- **AffinityFlow** (ICML 2025) — sequence-only **antibody affinity maturation** by alternating optimization: guide structure generation toward high binding affinity with a structure-based predictor, then inverse-fold to propose mutations refined by a sequence-based predictor; wraps AlphaFold inside flow matching. — [arXiv 2502.10365 (2025-02)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2502.10365)
- **"Mapping the evolution of computationally designed protein binders"** (2025-10) — affinity maturation by **beam search guided by ProteinMPNN + AF-Multimer rewards (iPAE)** and by continuous hypermutation from a single parental minibinder; finding: affinity-increasing mutations are **largely additive (minimal epistasis)** and many sit **away from the direct interface** — supports pose-preserving whole-scaffold maturation, not just interface tinkering. — [bioRxiv 2025.10.04.680454](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.10.04.680454.full.pdf)
- **BindEnergyCraft** (2025) — casts structure predictors as **energy-based models** to score/optimize binders; an alternative objective for a pose-anchored optimization loop. — [arXiv 2505.21241](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2505.21241)
- **AutoBinder Agent** (2026) — MCP-based agent orchestrating an end-to-end binder-design pipeline (generate→MPNN→fold→filter); relevant as the agentic wrapper pattern, not a new carryover mechanism. — [arXiv 2602.00019](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2602.00019)

### Inferences
- **The ΔΔG predictors are the most directly usable "pose-preserving affinity maturation" for the TNFα mini-binder** and the cleanest complement to ArcRefine: take the validated pH-7.4 bound complex, scan single/double mutants with RDE-PPI (binding) + ThermoMPNN/HERMES (stability/expression), keep the pose frozen, and rank mutations that raise affinity without introducing Cys or aggregation-prone runs. Because "Mapping the evolution…" shows affinity gains are additive and often off-interface, this pairs naturally with holding the interface fixed while sweeping the rest.
- **EvoPro and PPIFlow are the closest full-pipeline analogs to ArcRefine** (evolve/refine an existing bound design with an explicit anti-drift mechanism), and PPIFlow is strikingly parallel: fix interface residues, re-noise, flow-refine — a flow-matching restatement of "carry the pose, change the rest." Either could substitute for ArcRefine with a cleaner provenance than a 3-day-old single-author preprint.
- Antibody-specific tools (GearBind, RDE-PPI, AffinityFlow, RFantibody) were built for Ab/VHH, but the **ΔΔG ones (GearBind, RDE-PPI) score any protein–protein complex**, so they transfer to a mini-protein TNFα binder; AffinityFlow/RFantibody are less directly applicable (CDR/framework-shaped).
- None of these model pH/protonation — they preserve a *single* (pH-7.4) bound pose. The user's PROPKA/xTB/explicit-state-MD occupancy gate must still run downstream on any matured sequence to confirm the pH-6.0 release; carryover refinement and pH-selectivity are orthogonal and complementary.

### Gaps
- Did not fetch primary sources for FoldX / Rosetta Flex-ddG / FEP this round (well-established; cited generically). GearBind and ThermoMPNN exact repo URLs/licenses were inferred from search snippets (GearBind code likely under the DeepGraphLearning/Mila umbrella; ThermoMPNN at Kuhlman-Lab) — confirm repo + license before use.
- EvoPro's primary publication DOI/date not pinned here (repo + Tamarind describe it; original is a Kuhlman-lab 2023 paper — verify citation).
- PPIFlow's `10.64898` DOI prefix is non-standard for bioRxiv (normally `10.1101`); confirm the record is genuine and not a mirror artifact.

### Takeaway
