# Post-generation affinity/interface optimization and scoring/ranking methods (2024–2026) for de-novo TNF-α binders

Scope: methods that (1) mature/refine the affinity of an existing de-novo binder or (2) score/rank/triage candidates, each with code + license + inputs + GPU/CPU cost + whether it **IMPROVES** affinity (proposes better sequences) or only **RANKS** (scores what you give it). Our stack: Boltz-2, Mosaic, ProteinMPNN, AF2-initial-guess, OpenMM (Amber/GBn2), GFN2-xTB. Target ~150 residues, 2-day budget on 8×H100/CPU. Primary validated ranker already in use: AF2-IG `pae_interaction` (~6.2 on current leads).

---

## KQ1 — Physics interface/affinity-maturation and rescoring methods (code + 2-day feasibility)

### Takeaway
All the classic physics tools have released code and are feasible for a 150-residue complex in 2 days, but they almost all **RANK mutations** rather than propose them, and they span a cost/rigor spectrum: FoldX/Rosetta-ddG/xTB (seconds–minutes, empirical, scan hundreds) → flex ddG / MM-GBSA (minutes–hours, ensemble/end-state) → alchemical FEP/TI via perses (hours–day per mutation on GPU, the only rigorous ΔΔG, but sampling-limited for charge changes). The 2025 binder meta-analysis specifically endorses adding **Rosetta ΔG/ΔSASA + interface shape complementarity** on top of AF confidence for triage.

### Cited Findings
- **Flex ddG (Rosetta)** — ensemble ΔΔG of interface mutations: backrub sampling → side-chain repack → torsion min, averaged over an ensemble; benchmarked on 1,240 mutants, outperforms less-sampling methods, with gains on small→large side-chain and antibody-antigen mutations. RosettaScripts protocol; CPU; embarrassingly parallel (default ~35 backrub models/mutation). RANKS mutations. [bioRxiv preprint](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/221689.full.pdf); runner example [git.biohpc.swmed.edu flex_ddg_ala_scn_runner](https://git.biohpc.swmed.edu/s184069/flex_ddg_ala_scn_runner/-/tree/master); [tutorial mirror](https://huggingface.co/OneScience-Group/flex_ddG_tutorial)
- **Rosetta InterfaceAnalyzer** — interface metrics for filtering/ranking: `dG_separated` (binding ΔG), `dG_separated/dSASAx100` (binding energy per unit area; < −1.5 is "pretty good"), `dSASA_int` (buried SASA), shape complementarity / `packstat` (0–1; >0.65 good). Developed Lewis/Stranges/Adolf-Bryfogle (Kuhlman PI). CPU, minutes/model. RANKS / metrics. [Rosetta analyzing_interface_quality demo](https://docs.rosettacommons.org/demos/latest/public/analyzing_interface_quality/README); [InterfaceAnalyzer docs](https://rosettacommons.org/docs/latest/application_documentation/analysis/interface-analyzer)
- **FoldX 5.0** — empirical ΔΔG: `BuildModel` (mutate + build WT/mutant) then `AnalyseComplex` (interaction free energy between chains) gives interface ΔΔG. "Free for academic users" (proprietary binary, not open-source). CPU, ~seconds per run — can scan hundreds of mutations in 2 days. RANKS mutations; GearBind uses FoldX to build its mutant structures. [FoldX 5.0 paper (Bioinformatics)](https://academic.oup.com/bib/article/23/3/bbac074/6552273); [foldxsuite.crg.eu](https://foldxsuite.crg.eu/node/231)
- **gmx_MMPBSA (MM-GBSA/MM-PBSA)** — end-state free energy from MD trajectories; supports PB, GB, 3D-RISM; protein–protein and protein–ligand; computational alanine scanning, entropy corrections, per-residue decomposition. Built on AmberTools' `MMPBSA.py`, works with any GROMACS + AmberTools20/21. Free of charge. Needs a short MD run first (GPU) then cheap post-processing (CPU). RANKS / rescores (not rigorous absolute ΔG). [github Valdes-Tresanco-MS/gmx_MMPBSA](https://github.com/Valdes-Tresanco-MS/gmx_MMPBSA); [paper](https://pypi.org/project/gmx-MMPBSA/1.1.1/)
- **perses (alchemical RBFE for protein mutations)** — GPU-accelerated open-source relative binding free energy on OpenMM, explicitly designed to predict amino-acid mutation effects on protein:protein binding. On barnase:barstar with alchemical replica-exchange (+ solute tempering) and long sims: RMSE 1.61 kcal/mol (95% CI 1.12–2.11), 86% of estimates within 1 kcal/mol. Sampling is the bottleneck — charge-changing mutations have mutation-specific slow degrees of freedom. The only rigorous ΔΔG here; hours–day per mutation per GPU. RANKS (validates which mutation genuinely improves). [github choderalab/perses](https://github.com/choderalab/perses); [sampling-challenges study (PMC11219094)](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11219094/)
- **OpenFE (Open Free Energy)** — permissively-licensed open-source Python framework for RBFE on OpenMM (HREX, SAMS, non-equilibrium switching). Benchmarked on ~200 **ligand** transformations; roadmap lists ABFE and protein–ligand curation. Protein-mutation RBFE is the perses niche, not OpenFE's primary path. [OpenFE toolkit paper (Zenodo record)](https://zenodo.org/records/8158671)
- **GFN2-xTB interface interaction energy** — semiempirical tight-binding, non-covalent interaction energies for systems ~1000 atoms; best-in-class among semiempirical QM for protein-ligand binding (MAE ~4.9 kcal/mol neutral, ~10.25 kcal/mol charged ligands). Open-source (`xtb`, grimme-lab). Seconds–minutes per structure, but a ~150-res complex (~2,300 atoms) exceeds the comfortable size → truncate to the interface cluster + implicit solvent (matches our existing dE_switch usage). RANKS (relative interface energy). [GFN2-xTB method paper collection](https://acs.figshare.com/collections/GFN2-xTB_An_Accurate_and_Broadly_Parametrized_Self-Consistent_Tight-Binding_Quantum_Chemical_Method_with_Multipole_Electrostatics_and_Density-Dependent_Dispersion_Contributions/4395254); protein-ligand QM-screening use [BIOI 2025](https://journal.hep.com.cn/bioi/EN/10.15212/bioi-2025-0071)

### Inferences
- **2-day feasibility verdict:** FoldX, Rosetta InterfaceAnalyzer/FastRelax, and GFN2-xTB interface clusters are all trivially 2-day-feasible (scan hundreds of point mutants). Flex ddG and MM-GBSA are feasible for tens of candidates given 8×H100 (MD) + CPU parallelism. perses FEP is feasible only for a handful of carefully chosen mutations in 2 days — reserve it as the confirmatory tier.
- MM-GBSA fits our stack natively: we already run OpenMM (Amber/GBn2), so MM-GBSA can be done directly in OpenMM (AmberTools `MMPBSA.py` route) without adding GROMACS; gmx_MMPBSA is the GROMACS wrapper if we prefer its tooling/decomposition.
- Every physics method here preserves the binding mode by construction (you mutate ≤ a few interface side chains and re-score on the fixed backbone), so they are inherently safe for keeping a His pH-switch — just exclude the switch residue(s) from the mutable set and re-score each mutant at both protonation states and against both human and mouse TNF.

### Gaps
- Exact SPDX licenses for gmx_MMPBSA (repo states GPL), perses, and xtb not re-verified this pass (all are confirmed free/open-source).
- No cited wall-clock for perses on a 150-res interface specifically (only the barnase:barstar accuracy benchmark); the "hours–day/mutation" figure is a standard-practice inference, not a sourced number for our system.

## KQ2 — Learned rankers/scorers for de-novo binders 2024–2026 (code + trustworthiness for de-novo PPI)

### Takeaway
Two distinct classes: (a) **confidence-based rankers** that read AF2/AF3/Boltz PAE and are validated on *de-novo* binders — **ipSAE** is the current best, endorsed by a 3,766-binder meta-analysis; and (b) **learned ΔΔG regressors** (PPIformer, GearBind) trained on SKEMPI/PPIRef — excellent for ranking *point mutations* on an existing interface but trained on *natural* complexes, so best used for local maturation ranking, not absolute de-novo interface quality. Boltz-2's affinity head is confirmed weak for protein-protein (a Dec-2025 study shows it underperforms sequence-based models), consistent with our own finding.

### Cited Findings
- **2025 de-novo binder meta-analysis (3,766 binders, 15 targets)** — re-predicts each binder:target with AF2 (initial-guess + ColabFold), AF3, and Boltz-1; finds **AF3-derived ipSAE** the top discriminator, **+1.4× average precision over ipAE** (the metric RFdiffusion pipelines usually filter on). Combining AF3 ipSAE with **Rosetta ΔG/ΔSASA + interface shape complementarity** further improves precision; simple linear models on a few AF3 features generalize across datasets. Recommends target-agnostic filtering = AF3 ipSAE_min ranking + structural filters. Performance varies by target. [bioRxiv 2025.08.14.670059](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.08.14.670059v1)
- **ipSAE** (interprotein Score from Aligned Errors) — fixes ipTM's dilution over disordered/accessory regions by using only residue pairs with good interchain PAE and adjusting d0; reads AF2/AF3/Boltz-1 output JSON with no code changes; separates true/false complexes better than ipTM. Open-source, freely available. CPU, effectively instant. RANKS (confidence). [github dunbracklab/IPSAE](https://github.com/dunbracklab/IPSAE); [bioRxiv 2025.02.10.637595](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.02.10.637595.full.pdf)
- **de_novo_binder_scoring (DigBioLab)** — ready-made MIT-licensed triage pipeline computing exactly the meta-analysis stack: Rosetta interface energy + buried SASA, AF2/ColabFold/Boltz/AF3 confidence, **ipSAE + ipPAE**, DockQ, RMSD, and PyMOL metrics (H-bonds, BSA, contacts). Analysis scripts run on CPU (GPU only for the structure prediction); input = PDB with binder as chain A, or sequence CSV. Needs PyRosetta license. RANKS / triage. [github DigBioLab/de_novo_binder_scoring](https://github.com/DigBioLab/de_novo_binder_scoring)
- **PPIformer** — SE(3)-equivariant transformer, pre-trained on PPIRef (coarse-grained structural masked modeling), fine-tuned on SKEMPI v2.0 with a thermodynamic log-odds loss; predicts ΔΔG of single/multi-point mutations on PPIs; validated by recovering known favorable mutations (staphylokinase thrombolytic, anti-SARS-CoV-2 antibody). License **MIT**; GPU or CPU; Python 3.10 + PyTorch; input = PDB + mutation codes (e.g., `SC16A`); fast (sub-second–seconds/mutation). RANKS / can suggest maturation mutations. [github anton-bushuiev/PPIformer](https://github.com/anton-bushuiev/PPIformer); [paper (ICLR'24)](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2310.18515); [CPU web demo](https://anton-bushuiev-ppiformer-cpu.hf.space/)
- **GearBind** — pretrainable geometric GNN (GearNet, multi-relational/multi-level message passing), contrastive pretraining on CATH, fine-tuned on SKEMPI; outperforms prior SOTA on SKEMPI + an independent set. Wet-lab validated: CR3022 vs Omicron spike up to **17-fold** affinity gain, a human sdAb vs 5T4 up to **5.6-fold**. License **Apache-2.0**; A100 GPU, ~2 min/mutation; needs **FoldX-generated** WT+mutant PDBs + a CSV of mutations/chains; pretrained weights via gdown. RANKS (then you build the top-ranked mutants → genuine maturation). [github DeepGraphLearning/GearBind](https://github.com/DeepGraphLearning/GearBind); [Nat. Commun. 2024 / bioRxiv](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2023.08.10.552845v3)
- **Boltz-2 affinity head — weak for PPI (confirmed):** adapting Boltz-2's protein-ligand affinity module to protein-protein (TCR3d, PPB-affinity) underperforms sequence-based models in both small and large data regimes; the bottleneck is the affinity module extracting thermodynamics from the structural representation, not coordinate accuracy; structure embeddings are complementary to (not a replacement for) sequence embeddings. [arXiv 2512.06592 (Dec 2025)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.06592v1)

### Inferences
- **Trust for de-novo PPI:** confidence rankers (ipSAE, AF2-IG `pae_interaction`, ipPAE) are the ones *validated on de-novo binders* (the meta-analysis population) — trust these for binary binder-vs-non-binder triage. The learned ΔΔG regressors (PPIformer, GearBind) are trained on SKEMPI/PPIRef *natural* complexes and on *mutation effects*, so they are reliable for ranking small perturbations around an already-good interface (relative maturation) but should not be trusted as an absolute de-novo interface-quality score — use them downstream of AF2-IG/ipSAE triage, not instead of it.
- Our existing finding (Boltz-2 affinity weak for PPI; AF2-IG pae the validated primary) is corroborated by arXiv 2512.06592 and the meta-analysis — keep AF2-IG/ipSAE as the primary ranker; Boltz-2 affinity is at best a weak orthogonal signal.
- `de_novo_binder_scoring` is the lowest-effort high-value adoption: it packages the meta-analysis-endorsed stack (ipSAE + Rosetta ΔG/ΔSASA + SC) and runs analysis on CPU; near-zero integration risk given we already run AF2-IG/Boltz/PyRosetta.

### Gaps
- ipSAE repository's explicit license text not confirmed this pass (described as "freely available"; code is also vendored in third-party packages, e.g. `molecular_simulations.analysis.ipSAE`).
- No head-to-head of PPIformer vs GearBind vs flex ddG vs FoldX *on de-novo (non-natural) interfaces* was found — their SKEMPI benchmarks are on natural complexes; transferability to de-novo interfaces is unquantified.
- AlphaProteo (DeepMind 2024) is a relevant learned de-novo binder generator/scorer but was not confirmed to have open code/weights this pass — treat as not runnable on our stack unless verified.

## KQ3 — ArcRefine-like sequence-optimization affinity maturation that PRESERVES the binding mode (other groups)

### Takeaway
"ArcRefine-like" = differentiable/iterative sequence optimization against a structure-prediction oracle while holding the complex/binding mode. We already own the strongest member of this class — **Mosaic** (Escalante Bio): JAX, gradient optimization over a relaxed sequence space combining multiple expert models (Boltz-1, ESM-2, ProteinMPNN) into one loss. The main *other-group* options are **BoltzDesign1** (differentiable Boltz — the direct ArcRefine analog), **EvoPro** (genetic-algorithm AF2+MPNN optimizer, binding-mode-preserving by loss design), and plain **ProteinMPNN interface-redesign on the fixed backbone** re-ranked by AF2-IG. These IMPROVE (propose new sequences) but only preserve the binding mode if explicitly constrained (fix hotspot/His residues, restrict the mutable set, keep the target pose).

### Cited Findings
- **Mosaic (Escalante Bio)** — modular composite-objective design in JAX; gradient ascent/descent over a continuous relaxed sequence space, combining multiple expert models (e.g., Boltz-1, ESM-2, ProteinMPNN) in one loss to co-optimize binding, solubility, thermostability. On the Adaptyv Nipah de-novo target: **89% hit-rate on 9 designs (8 binders)**. Noted as high-ceiling but needs objective tuning and standard downstream filtering (often produces designs that fail simple in-silico tests). IMPROVES. [github escalante-bio/mosaic](https://www.sourcepulse.org/projects/31116087); [Adaptyv skill writeup](https://www.skills.sh/adaptyvbio/protein-design-skills/mosaic)
- **BoltzDesign1** — the direct ArcRefine analog from another group: inverts the all-atom Boltz structure predictor for binder design by enabling gradient flow between the Pairformer and the confidence module (loss backprop from confidence to inputs), and limits recycling to a single refinement step to cut memory while preserving gradient quality. IMPROVES (gradient sequence optimization against Boltz). [bioRxiv 2025.04.06.647261](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.04.06.647261v1)
- **EvoPro (Kuhlman Lab)** — genetic algorithm: iterative rounds of AF2 structure prediction + sequence diversification (random mutagenesis, crossover, or ProteinMPNN) to evolve tight binders. Scores on three AF2-derived terms: placement confidence (interface PAE), fold confidence (pLDDT), and a conformational-stability term minimizing the bound-vs-unbound difference — i.e., it *optimizes while rewarding a consistent bound pose*. Reported picomolar–nanomolar binders. IMPROVES; compute-heavy (many AF2 calls/generation — maps well to 8×H100). [github Kuhlman-Lab/evopro](https://github.com/Kuhlman-Lab/evopro); [paper (PMC10710080)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10710080/)
- **BindCraft** — AF2-backpropagation hallucination + ProteinMPNN redesign + PyRosetta filtering (AF2 confidence thresholds + Rosetta ddG). License **MIT**, but requires a **PyRosetta license** (free academic, paid commercial). Primarily *de-novo* generation rather than maturation, but its backprop + MPNN + filter loop is reusable; IMPROVES. [github martinpacesa/BindCraft](https://github.com/martinpacesa/BindCraft); [Rosetta tutorial](https://rosettacommons.org/2025/07/24/new-bindcraft-tutorial-now-available/)

### Inferences
- **Binding-mode / pH-switch / cross-reactivity preservation is a usage constraint, not a free property.** All the generative optimizers (Mosaic, BoltzDesign1, EvoPro, BindCraft/MPNN) will drift off the current binding mode unless you (a) fix the His switch residue(s) and any validated hotspots, (b) restrict the mutable set to non-switch interface positions, and (c) make the objective **multi-state** — sum losses over human-TNF + mouse-TNF and over pH 7.4 (bound) + pH 6.0 (released). Mosaic's multi-model loss and EvoPro's bound-vs-unbound term are built for exactly this kind of multi-term constraint; this is the ArcRefine-like path that can improve affinity *without* breaking the switch.
- Caution grounded in our own record (AF2 binding is sequence-specific; wholesale MPNN re-seq of a binding backbone can kill binding): prefer **point-mutation maturation** (scan a few interface positions, re-fold with AF2-IG, confirm `pae_interaction`/ipSAE hold or improve) over aggressive full-interface re-design. The scoring tools in KQ1/KQ2 (flex ddG, FoldX, PPIformer, GearBind, perses) are the safest maturation drivers because they operate on the fixed backbone and only rank the mutations you choose.

- **Top 3 most useful for maturing/triaging our TNF-α binders (2-day, no switch/cross-react breakage):**
  1. **ipSAE + Rosetta ΔG/ΔSASA + interface SC via `de_novo_binder_scoring`** (MIT; CPU analysis; meta-analysis-endorsed) — immediate, reliable *triage* upgrade over raw `pae_interaction`/ipTM; re-score every lead against human AND mouse TNF at both pH states. RANKS.
  2. **PPIformer and/or GearBind point-mutation ΔΔG scan** (MIT / Apache-2.0; fast GPU) over non-switch interface positions → short-list of affinity-improving mutations that keep the backbone, His switch, and pose; confirm the top few by re-folding with AF2-IG and (optionally) perses FEP. Bridges RANK→IMPROVE with minimal pose risk.
  3. **Mosaic multi-state constrained optimization** (already on our stack) — fix His + hotspots, mutable set = non-switch interface, loss summed over human/mouse × pH7.4/pH6.0. The highest-ceiling *improver*; use BoltzDesign1 or EvoPro as alternative differentiable/GA engines if Mosaic's objective is hard to tune. IMPROVES (must be constrained + filtered through #1).

### Gaps
- Licenses for Mosaic, BoltzDesign1, and EvoPro repos not individually confirmed this pass (all public/open-source; SPDX unverified).
- "ArcRefine" itself was not located as a named public tool in search (likely very recent/internal); the differentiable-Boltz refiner *class* is represented here by BoltzDesign1 and our own Mosaic. If ArcRefine specifically is needed, its repo/paper was not found.
- No source quantifies how well any of these preserve a His-mediated pH switch specifically — multi-state constraint is a design recommendation, not a benchmarked result.
