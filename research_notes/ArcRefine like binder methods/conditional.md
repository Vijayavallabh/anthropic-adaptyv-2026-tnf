# Conditional / pH-switchable / state-selective binder design methods & tools (2024–2026)

Scope: catalog of methods/tools for designing or scoring CONDITIONAL binders (pH, state-selective, negative/multistate, allosteric, logic), each with code availability + license + inputs + runnability-in-~2-days + relevance to the TNF-α task (BIND pH 7.4, RELEASE pH 6.0, i.e. protonation breaks binding; plus mouse cross-reactivity). Our current stack = single/2-His interface switch scored by PROPKA Wyman linkage + explicit two-state MD; we already hold JustHISpKa and know Baker 2025 + ArcRefine.

**TOP 3 MOST ADOPTABLE (bottom line up front):**
1. **Reimplement the Baker-2025 Rosetta pH-filter layer** (pH_score + `fa_elec` dddG(pH5−pH7) ≥ 0 filter + His–cationic-distance enrichment). No code released, but fully described, and it is the only published pipeline that experimentally validated low-pH RELEASE on a TNF-superfamily receptor (TNFR2, 122× weaker at pH 5.4) using exactly our RFdiffusion+ProteinMPNN family. ~1–2 days of PyRosetta to bolt onto existing designs.
2. **HBNet (Rosetta/PyRosetta)** to install cooperative, buried multi-His H-bond networks (monomer-destabilization + sharper transition). Open, free for academics, worked example notebook exists.
3. **Expand the pKa sign-consensus**: keep JustHISpKa (best-in-class for His, MUE ~0.4) + PROPKA3 + pKAI, and ADD DeepKa (cpHMD-trained, different method-class; literature-recommended His consensus is DeepKa+PROPKA3). pip/web installable, ~hours.

Honorable mentions: SIpHAB (NRC, sequence-based, explicitly parametrized for acidic RELEASE — but antibody-Fv only); AlloGen/TD3B (ChatterjeeLab, code released, state-selective generative design — but agonist/antagonist, not pH).

## Key Question 1 — Tools/code for pH-switch design or His-pKa engineering (pKa predictors, constant-pH MD, Rosetta pH scoring, explicit-state / negative-design frameworks)

### Takeaway
The best His-specific pKa tool is the one we already have (JustHISpKa, MUE ~0.4); the literature-recommended orthogonal partner is DeepKa (cpHMD-class) + PROPKA3; Rosetta pH scoring should be done the Baker way (geometric/`fa_elec` proxy at pH5-vs-pH7), NOT via Rosetta's titrating pH_mode (which "refused to finish" in an independent benchmark, consistent with our "pH_mode is dead" finding); constant-pH MD tooling exists (GROMACS+phbuilder 2024, Amber pmemd, Force Field X/AMOEBA) but is a poor fit for our uv/https env.

### Cited Findings
- **JustHISpKa** (NRC Canada, Human Health Therapeutics Molecular Modeling Team): structure-based His pKa predictor, **MUE 0.4 pH units** across 91 histidines / 38 structures (PKAD), using Amber FF electrostatics + continuum solvent + limited rotamer sampling + buried-sidechain empirical correction; runs online; inputs = Tripos **mol2** + Amber **prmtop**; built explicitly "to support pH-responsive protein design" — [paper (PMC11815838)](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11815838/); [software page](https://mm.nrc-cnrc.gc.ca/software/JustHISpKa). We already hold `justhispka-1.0.2.tgz` + `justHisPKa.lic` in the repo root.
- **Independent high-throughput pKa benchmark (7 methods)** — for the 41 histidines in the set, ranking was **DeepKa (RMSE 0.89, R² 0.43) > H++ > pKAI+ > DelPhiPKa**; best strategy was a **consensus of DeepKa + PROPKA3**; "Rosetta-pKa could not be benchmarked due to it refusing to finish computations" — [Comparative Performance of High-Throughput Methods for Protein pKa Predictions (PMC10466379)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10466379/). (Note: JustHISpKa's MUE 0.4 beats DeepKa's MeUE 0.61 and H++'s 0.47 for His, so JustHISpKa remains our SOTA single tool.)
- **PypKa** — Poisson-Boltzmann continuum-electrostatics pKa; open source, inputs = PDB or UniProt code; web server at pypka.org — [GitHub mms-fcul/PypKa](https://github.com/mms-fcul/PypKa).
- **pKAI / pKAI+** — ML pKa predictors trained on ~6M PypKa values from ~50k structures; ~1000× faster than PB; `pip install pKAI` — [PyPI pkai](https://pypi.org/project/pkai); [method paper (chemrxiv)](https://chemrxiv.org/engage/chemrxiv/article-details/63d3c2d910cb6a14c0e88bfa).
- **DeepKa** — deep-learning pKa trained/validated on **continuous constant-pH MD** of 279 soluble proteins; accuracy "close to cpHMD benchmarking"; ACS Omega 2021; web server (Xiamen Univ.) — [method (chemrxiv)](https://chemrxiv.org/engage/chemrxiv/article-details/61570a1bcc53d94e30b901bd). Exact GitHub/license not confirmed (see Gaps).
- **Constant-pH MD tooling (2024)**: a KTH 2024 thesis implemented **continuous constant-pH in GROMACS** plus **phbuilder** (Python setup automator for cpHMD runs) — [KTH thesis listing](https://www.kth.se/en/forskning/kalender/constant-ph-molecular-dynamics-and-applications-1.1337907). **Amber pmemd** has GPU continuous-cpHMD (GB implicit; pKa RMSD 0.80, r 0.83); **Force Field X** has cpHMD compatible with AMOEBA polarizable multipoles (open source) — [AMOEBA cpHMD (chemrxiv)](https://chemrxiv.org/engage/chemrxiv/article-details/652eafa745aaa5fdbb3429dc); [cpHMD overview (PMC6934042)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6934042).
- **Rosetta pH scoring (the usable path)** — Baker 2025 did NOT titrate; they scored an ad hoc **pH_score** (weighted Rosetta H-bond terms distinguishing His as acceptor/donor/doubly-protonated-donor, surface vs core, bb vs sc, at pH 5 vs pH 7) and a **dddG_elec = `fa_elec`(pH5) − `fa_elec`(pH7)** filter keeping designs with ddG_elec ≥ 0 (electrostatics unfavorable at low pH), plus a PyRosetta script enumerating shortest His↔cationic (His/Lys/Arg) distances — [Baker 2025 (PMC12621740)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12621740/).

### Inferences
- Our pKa stack is already strong; the cheapest upgrade is adding **DeepKa** as a third orthogonal method-class (cpHMD-trained) to our existing PROPKA + pKAI, since DeepKa+PROPKA3 is the benchmarked His consensus and JustHISpKa stays as the high-accuracy anchor. This directly strengthens the "sign-consensus" we already run.
- Baker's `fa_elec` pH5-vs-pH7 dddG and His-cationic-distance scripts are a **cheap, high-throughput geometric/electrostatic pre-filter** that is fully complementary to our (more rigorous but expensive) PROPKA-Wyman-linkage + two-state-MD confirmatory gate. The clean integration: Rosetta proxy to rank thousands → PROPKA/JustHISpKa/MD to confirm the top tail.
- Constant-pH MD tooling is real and improving, but given our prior finding that constant-pH MD / alchemical FEP are a NO-GO in the uv/https env, the pragmatic substitute remains our fixed-protonation explicit two-state MD; treat GROMACS-cpHMD/phbuilder as "nice if the env allowed it," not a 2-day adoption.

### Gaps
- Could not verify DeepKa's exact code repo/license/runnability (web server exists; ACS Omega 2021 supplement not opened).
- No dedicated open-source "Ising / Wyman-linkage cooperative-titration scoring" package surfaced (see KQ3) — cooperativity appears to be engineered structurally rather than scored by a packaged tool.

## Key Question 2 — 2024–2026 de-novo conditional/switchable binder papers WITH code beyond Baker 2025 (LOCKR/Co-LOCKR, allosteric, chemically-switchable, SIpHAB-like predictors)

### Takeaway
There is NO other released de-novo pH-RELEASE binder pipeline besides the (unreleased) Baker 2025 one; the nearest released tools are either (a) sequence-based His-mutation predictors for antibodies (SIpHAB, free, but Fv-only and we don't use antibodies) or (b) state-selective generative frameworks with code (AlloGen/TD3B) that target agonist/antagonist, not pH. FoldArc/ArcRefine is a proprietary service with one Zenodo preprint and no code.

### Cited Findings
- **Baker 2025, "Computational design of pH-sensitive binders"** — RFdiffusion backbone + ProteinMPNN with **histidine bias (0.5/1.0/1.5)** + AF2 (pae, iRMSD) filtering; two strategies: **interface destabilization** (electrostatic repulsion; "all pH-dependent binders contained **two or more contacts between protonated His and cationic residues** [His/Lys/Arg]") and **monomer destabilization** (2–3-residue buried His/His–Arg–Lys H-bond networks via two PyRosetta scripts). Validated **low-pH RELEASE**: EphA2 up to **1000× weaker at pH 5.4**, **TNFR2 122× weaker**, IL-6 6× weaker; BLI = associate pH7.4 / dissociate pH5.4. **No Code/Data Availability section, no GitHub/Zenodo; code NOT released** (supplement is a single PDF) — [bioRxiv 2025.09.29.678932](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.09.29.678932) / [PMC12621740](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12621740/); summary [prelights](https://prelights.biologists.com/highlights/computational-design-of-ph-sensitive-binders/).
- **SIpHAB (NRC Canada, 2023/2024)** — **sequence-based** predictor of His mutations in the antibody variable region giving pH-biased antigen binding; trained on dual-pH His-scanning of ~3,490 antibody–antigen complexes; **explicitly parametrized for either direction — toward acidic binding (tumor targeting) OR against acidic binding (antigen RELEASE in the endosome)**; input = antibody primary sequence only; "freely available for academic and commercial purposes" — [software/runner](https://mm.nrc-cnrc.gc.ca/software/siphab/runner); paper in [mAbs (Dec 2024), DOAJ](https://doaj.org/article/bd1f5d5978474d8ab00891f2829d0515).
- **AlloGen / TD3B (ChatterjeeLab, 2025–26)** — "Conformation-Selective Binder Design with Differential State Scoring"; decouples backbone generation from a learned SE(3)-invariant interface-graph state-selectivity scorer; **TD3B** = Transition-Directed Discrete Diffusion generating peptide binders with specified **agonist/antagonist** behavior (Direction Oracle + soft affinity gate + fine-tuned discrete-diffusion MDLM). **Code + checkpoints released** (CLI `inference.py` / `finetune_on_target.py`, Colab T4 demo; PyTorch+RDKit; ~3.4 GB checkpoints via Google Drive); inputs = target sequence(s) + direction. **No explicit license stated.** Does NOT address pH; docs give "no indication" it is readily pH-adaptable — [HuggingFace ChatterjeeLab/TD3B](https://huggingface.co/ChatterjeeLab/TD3B); [AlloGen @ ICML 2026](https://icml.cc/virtual/2026/70800). (A paper id of "arXiv 2605.09810" was reported by the repo page but is unverified.)
- **FoldArc / ArcRefine** — single preprint "ArcRefine improves de novo binder designs while preserving binding modes," Ken Osumi, **Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.23115832 (2026, unreviewed)**; "ArcRefine" = proprietary sequence-update-preserving-binding-mode technique inside a custom design *service*; **no GitHub, no open-source tool, no pH/conditional content** — [foldarc.com](https://foldarc.com) / [paper page](https://foldarc.com/research/structural-carryover/paper/). (This is the item we already knew; it is not a conditional-binder method.)
- **LOCKR / Co-LOCKR** — de-novo colocalization-dependent switches doing AND/OR/NOT logic (2019–2020); 2024 extension = LOCKR-based intracellular Ras-activity sensor. Intracellular conformational/logic switches, NOT pH binders — [Co-LOCKR (IPD)](https://www.ipd.uw.edu/?p=5553); [LOCKR (Science aba6527)](https://www.ipd.uw.edu/publication-pdfs/173/2dcae20fb11774d008b66d8583a2176d/science.aba6527.pdf).
- **De novo design of allosterically switchable protein assemblies (ISTA/Baker-adjacent)** — rigid-body coupling of peptide-switchable hinge modules to interfaces to direct alternative oligomeric states (rings/cages) — [ISTA research-explorer 17463](https://research-explorer.ista.ac.at/record/17463). (Our prior note already flagged hinged-lid switches as valid-readout-but-unbuildable here.)
- **Chemically-switchable (context, not pH):** Chemically-Disruptable Heterodimers / AIR switches (drug-dissociated ON/OFF), Nat Commun 2021 — [PMC8486872](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC8486872/); EPFL PhD thesis "Computational Design of Protein Switches" (Buckley, 2025; small-molecule-gated binders validated in cells) — [EPFL infoscience](https://infoscience.epfl.ch/items/2afd965a-1871-4187-8a9f-6b740ef0530a); protease-activated autoinhibited antagonists via AF2+ProteinMPNN (conditional activation).
- **Protease/membrane context pH precedent:** Goldbach et al., "De novo design of monomeric helical bundles for pH-controlled membrane lysis," Protein Science 2023 — buried His H-bond networks drive acid-triggered conformational change (monomer-destabilization blueprint) — [PubMed 37632837](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37632837/).

### Inferences
- The field's only experimentally-validated, direction-correct (bind 7.4 / release low-pH) de-novo mini-binder pipeline is Baker 2025, and it is built on exactly our generator family (RFdiffusion + ProteinMPNN). Even without their code, their recipe is the single highest-value thing to port, and their TNFR2 result (TNF-superfamily receptor) is near-direct evidence that de-novo low-pH release is achievable on our target class.
- SIpHAB is the only released tool explicitly tuned for the RELEASE direction, but because it is antibody-Fv-sequence-based it only applies to us if we frame a binder as a VHH/scFv; it is a triage predictor, not a de-novo designer.
- AlloGen/TD3B is the strongest "released code for state-selective design" find and worth watching for its differential two-state scorer idea, but it is peptide/agonist-antagonist-focused and not pH-aware, so adoption for TNF pH-release would be a research project, not a 2-day lift; also no license (legal caution).

### Gaps
- SIpHAB runner page failed to load (TLS "unable to verify the first certificate" on the NRC gov host) — inputs/license confirmed only from search snippets + the mAbs paper, not first-hand.
- Did not confirm whether any 2025–26 bioRxiv preprint has released a *non-antibody* de-novo pH-release pipeline with code (searches returned only Baker 2025 unreleased + 2023 IPD membrane-lysis work).

## Key Question 3 — Methods to make a pH switch SHARPER/STRONGER (cooperative multi-His, Ising/linkage scoring, ultrasensitivity)

### Takeaway
Sharpness/cooperativity is achieved structurally, not by a packaged scorer: preorganize MULTIPLE buried His in H-bond networks (HBNet) and surround them with hydrophobic packing — transition pH and Hill-like cooperativity scale with the NUMBER of His networks and the strength of the hydrophobic environment. Baker 2025's own data (≥2 His–cation contacts in every working design) is consistent with "one His is often not enough."

### Cited Findings
- **Tunable pH-dependent cooperative switches (IPD/Baker, Science 2019, aav7897)** — designed homotrimers/heterodimers stable above pH 6.5 that undergo **cooperative, large-scale** collapse when buried network His protonate; **"the transition pH and cooperativity can be controlled through the number of histidine-containing networks and the strength of the surrounding hydrophobic interactions"**; more interfacial His → more cooperative collapse — [IPD summary](https://www.ipd.uw.edu/2019/05/tunable-ph-dependent-assemblies); [Janelia listing](https://www.janelia.org/publication/de-novo-design-of-tunable-ph-driven-conformational-changes); PDB 6MSQ.
- **HBNet mover (Rosetta/PyRosetta; Boyken et al., Science 2016)** — exhaustively enumerates closed buried H-bond networks, scores/ranks them, and seeds them into a scaffold with constraints; "pH-responsive hydrogen bond networks can be positioned in the core of protein bundles using the HBNet mover"; **open (RosettaCommons license; PyRosetta free for academics)**, with a worked notebook — [HBNet paper (IPD PDF)](https://www.ipd.uw.edu/publication-pdfs/42/89d580f79a3831ed81abefcd08d55d56/rapid-sampling-of-hydrogen-bond-networks-for-computational-protein-design.pdf); [HBNetMover docs](https://docs.rosettacommons.org/docs/latest/scripting_documentation/RosettaScripts/Movers/movers_pages/HBNetMover); [PyRosetta notebook 06.05](https://nbviewer.org/github/RosettaCommons/PyRosetta.notebooks/blob/master/notebooks/06.05-HBNet-Before-Design.ipynb).
- **Baker 2025 cooperativity signal** — every working pH-dependent binder had **≥2 protonated-His↔cationic contacts**; monomer-destabilization used **2–3-residue** buried networks (not single His); authors flag that finer pKa control near pH 6.5 "by tuning of the surrounding residues" is still future work (no explicit cooperativity-sharpening mechanism solved) — [PMC12621740](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12621740/).

### Inferences
- The actionable lever for a sharper 6.0→7.4 switch is **multi-His cooperativity**: move from single/2-His toward a small buried His network (2–3 His) built with HBNet, tuning transition pH via surrounding hydrophobic burial, exactly as the 2019 tunable-assemblies paper parameterized it. This is consistent with Baker 2025's ≥2-contact empirical rule and with our prior finding that "far-better needs Hill n>1 but stacking fights novelty" — i.e., cooperativity and novelty trade off, so add His networks sparingly.
- Wyman linkage (what we already integrate from PROPKA) IS the correct thermodynamic read-out of cooperative proton uptake; no separate Ising package is needed — score cooperativity as the steepness of Δn_H(pH) from the multi-method pKa set, and realize it structurally via HBNet.

### Gaps
- No open-source "ultrasensitivity/Hill-coefficient predictor for His networks" tool found; the quantitative transition-sharpness still has to come from our own two-state MD + linkage integral, not a packaged scorer.
- The 2019 tunable-assemblies cooperativity result is on homo-oligomer *assemblies*, not binder interfaces; transfer of the "number-of-networks → cooperativity" scaling to a binder interface is an inference, not a measured binder result.
