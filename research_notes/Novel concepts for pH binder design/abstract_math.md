# Abstract Mathematics for a De-Novo pH-Switchable TNF-α Binder

Scope note: this covers abstract/underused mathematical frameworks and how to operationalize each with the project's tools (RFdiffusion3, ProteinMPNN, AF2-initial-guess, Boltz-2, OpenMM MD, GFN2-xTB, PROPKA). Project-internal facts (single-His modest switch, RFd3 breaking the flat-polar wall, "OFF-state governs constitutivity," "fixed-charge MD is chemistry-biased → use xTB") come from the task brief and the project memory and are flagged as such (no external URL); every external claim carries a source.

## Q1 — Which areas of math could improve (a) sharp/cooperative pH transitions, (b) flat-polar binding geometry, (c) two-state selectivity, (d) designable novelty

### Takeaway
The single highest-leverage idea for a *drastic* switch upgrade is to stop designing one titratable site and instead design a **coupled cluster of protonatable sites tuned to a cooperative (Hill n_H>1) collapse** — this is the intersection of lattice statistical mechanics (Ising/binding-polynomial), catastrophe/bifurcation theory, and Wyman linkage. For the flat-polar-surface problem the leverage is in **surface-geometry math** (3D Zernike descriptors, geodesic/differential-geometry surface fingerprints, optimal transport of polar-group measures). Selectivity is best hardened by **multistate/negative (game-theoretic minimax) design plus kinetic (Markov-state/large-deviation) readouts**; novelty by **topological data analysis + a rate-distortion framing** of the novelty-vs-binding trade-off.

### Cited Findings

#### (a) Sharp / cooperative pH transitions — Hill n_H > 1
- A single titratable group gives a Henderson–Hasselbalch curve with Hill coefficient n_H = 1 (a "modest" ~10×/pH-unit ceiling); sharp biological switches require n_H > 1, which arises from **cooperativity between coupled sites**, and the Hill coefficient is the standard scalar measure of that cooperativity — the proton binding capacitance (dn̄_H/dpH) shows electrostatic coupling even more clearly than n_H — [Coupled protonation / capacitance](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2144168); [pepkalc coupled-protonation tool](https://st-protein02.chem.au.dk/pepkalc/).
- **An Ising model with a few microscopic protonation constants plus short-ranged pair interactions quantitatively describes the titration of complex multi-site molecules** — i.e., coupled protonation is formally a spin system with a pairwise coupling matrix — [Coupled protonation PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2144168); [proton coupling categories](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3318114).
- The protonation behavior (and cooperativity) of poly-ionizable systems depends strongly on the **distance between ionizable groups**, demonstrated for hexaamines — i.e., coupling strength is a geometric design knob — [Distance between ionizable groups](https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlehtml/1999/cp/a907397c).
- **Bifurcation theory formally links the Hill coefficient to switch sharpness**: increasing n_H moves the system toward criticality (critical points shift, stable/unstable manifolds approach), and cooperativity is what converts a graded response into a bistable toggle via saddle-node bifurcations — [Toggle-switch bifurcations](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2509.24926); [Buffering variability near criticality](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2212.08600); [Role of cooperativity (p53–miR34)](https://portal.fis.leibniz-lsb.tum.de/en/publications/the-role-of-cooperativity-in-a-p53-mir34-dynamical-mathematical-m-3/); [Ultrasensitivity](https://arodes.hes-so.ch/record/1964/files/vinckenbosch_ultrasensitivity_2017.pdf).
- **Wyman linkage** gives the rigorous thermodynamic law: ∂(ln K_bind)/∂pH = −Δn_H, so ΔΔG_pH = RT·ln10·∫Δn_H(pH)·dpH; proton-linked binding free energy is pH-dependent exactly when binding takes up/releases protons, and fixed-protonation shortcuts cause >2 kcal/mol errors (must use constant-pH / linkage methods) — [Wyman & Gill, *Binding and Linkage*](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/816655/binding-and-linkage-by-jeffries-wyman-stanley-gill); [Constant-pH MD + Wyman binding polynomial](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4306499).

#### (b) Binding geometry on flat, polar surfaces
- **3D Zernike descriptors (3DZD)** give a rotation-invariant series expansion of a molecular surface; the LZerD/PatchSurfer family docks by matching the 3DZD of two surfaces (shape complementarity), and PL-PatchSurfer adds **electrostatic potential** to each surface patch via 3DZD — near-native poses found within top ranks in 74% of bound cases — [3DZD (BMC Bioinformatics)](https://bmcbioinformatics.biomedcentral.com/track/pdf/10.1186/1471-2105-10-407); [LZerD / PatchSurfer](https://bmcbioinformatics.biomedcentral.com/articles/10.1186/s12859-018-2043-3). A 2025 benchmark explicitly retrieves protein surfaces by **shape + electrostatic potential** — [SHREC 2025 surface retrieval](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2509.12976).
- **MaSIF** treats the solvent-excluded surface as a manifold with geometric (shape index, curvature) and chemical (Poisson–Boltzmann electrostatics, H-bond, hydrophobicity) features and learns, via **geodesic (differential-geometry) convolutions**, "interaction fingerprints"; demonstrated for PPI-site prediction and ultrafast surface scanning to propose complementary binders — the surface "abstracts the underlying sequence" and exposes exactly the polar/geometric features that contact a partner — [MaSIF (bioRxiv)](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/606202.full.pdf); [MaSIF (EPFL/Nat Methods)](https://infoscience.epfl.ch/record/273279).
- **Optimal transport** provides rotation/translation-invariant structural matching: OTMol poses atom-matching as a *fused supervised Gromov–Wasserstein* problem — a Wasserstein term matches atom labels/types and a Gromov–Wasserstein term preserves intra-molecular pairwise distances (matching spatially-close to spatially-close) — preserving chirality/connectivity better than RMSD aligners — [OTMol (GW for molecular comparison)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.01550). OT also builds peptide conformational distributions by minimizing an interaction-energy expectation — [OT peptide conformations](https://www.citedrive.com/en/discovery/optimal-transport-method-for-efficient-modeling-of-extended-peptide-conformations/).

#### (c) Selectivity between two states (bind pH 7.4 / release pH 6.0)
- **Multistate design (MSD) with negative design** selects one sequence against *several* states at once, explicitly choosing sequence elements to *discourage* undesired states; it can redesign a homodimer into an obligate heterodimer or make a promiscuous interface monospecific, and MSD reportedly beats single-state design by ~15% on a ligand-binding/conformational benchmark — [Rosetta MSF framework](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5484525); [ProteinInterfaceMSMover](https://docs.rosettacommons.org/docs/latest/scripting_documentation/RosettaScripts/Movers/movers_pages/ProteinInterfaceMSMover); [Controlling binding specificity](https://cris.huji.ac.il/en/publications/computational-methods-for-controlling-binding-specificity/); [MSD methods](https://ideas.repec.org/a/plo/pcbi00/1005600.html).
- **Markov state models (MSMs)** are the key technology for the *kinetics* of rare transitions between long-lived states (protein folding, ligand association/**dissociation**); a well-built MSM yields transition rates / mean-first-passage times, and **large-deviation / rare-event** methods estimate k_off and dominant unbinding pathways that plain equilibrium ΔG misses — but the dissociation rate can only be computed if every step of the dissociation has been sampled — [Rare-event kinetics](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1409.6439); [MSM timescale limits](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1708.03011).
- **Game-theoretic framing of proteins** (folding networks as a game among competing conformations/weak links) exists in the literature and supports adversarial/robust two-state formulations — [Game theory of proteins (Csermely)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/q-bio/0409030).

#### (d) Designable novelty
- **Persistent homology / TDA**: topological fingerprints of a structure (barcodes of components H0, loops H1, voids H2) predict protein–ligand binding, and *element-specific* PH (ESPH) recovers binding mechanism and even shows hydrophobic contributions extending ~40 Å; distances between designs are measured by bottleneck/Wasserstein distance between persistence diagrams — [ESPH binding](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1703.10982); [Persistent (directed flag) Laplacian for affinity](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.02596v1); [PH + dynamical distances for binding](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/1412.1394).
- **Rate-distortion / information theory**: designing an optimal "molecular code" is formally a rate-distortion problem — balancing code quality (distortion of typical messages) against cost (bits), and artificial-protein design has used information-minimization / entropy-maximization within functional constraints — [Rate-distortion molecular codes](https://arxiv.org/abs/1007.4471).

#### Cross-cutting energy-landscape / network math
- **Spectral graph theory of contact/dynamical networks** explains allostery as signal transmission across the structure without necessarily a conformational change; MD-weighted contact graphs plus coarse-grained graph spectral analysis (perturbation-response, signaling efficiency) localize how a local perturbation "resonates" globally — a validated tool for protein engineering — [Dynamical perturbation network analysis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6604606); [Protein structure networks review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7395124); [Graph spectral allostery](https://eprints.iisc.ac.in/84377/1/Pro_str_fun_bio_2023.pdf).
- **Energy-landscape theory / Morse-theoretic view**: landscapes are surveyed by their critical points (minima, transition states = saddles) and their connectivity (disconnectivity graphs / energy-link networks); the native funnel and barrier structure govern switchability — [Graph-theoretical energy landscape](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0812.0316); [Energy landscape funnel](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC22965).

### Inferences
- **Coupled multi-His cluster is the mechanistic key to a drastic switch.** A single interface His (project's current switch) is pinned at n_H≈1, so its maximum fold-change over the 7.4→6.0 window (1.4 pH units) is small — consistent with the observed "~2–20×, modest." Catastrophe/Ising/Wyman math all say the same thing from different angles: to get a *sharp* collapse you need |Δn_H|>1, i.e. 2–3 titratable sites that protonate cooperatively and break the interface *together*. The RSC hexaamine result (coupling set by inter-group distance) says the design lever is the geometric spacing of the His/acid sites.
- **"OFF-state governs constitutivity" (project memory) is precisely the negative-design / minimax objective.** A positive linkage integral only proves *direction*; a constitutive bridge can pass it yet bind both states. Framing design as a game where the OFF (pH-6, protonated) state is an adversary to be destabilized is the formal fix.
- **Flat polar targets reward invariant surface descriptors.** With no pocket, binding is won by polar/charge complementarity + buried area. 3DZD (shape+ESP) and OT (Gromov–Wasserstein of polar-group point clouds) both give *pose-invariant* complementarity scores and can also rank *which compact sub-patch* of 1TNF is least-flat/most-designable — aligning with the project memory "target ONE compact sub-site."
- **Kinetic selectivity may matter more than equilibrium.** "Sweeping"/FcRn-recycling-style release is a k_off phenomenon; an MSM/large-deviation k_off ratio between the two protonation states is a stronger selectivity readout than ΔΔG, and a natural upgrade of the project's existing "explicit-state MD occupancy" gate.

### Gaps
- No source found that applies **catastrophe/cusp theory directly to protonation-driven binder release** (the bifurcation literature is gene-regulatory toggles); the transfer is a sound analogy but unproven for interface protonation.
- No external source quantifies **how much Hill cooperativity is achievable from an engineered surface His cluster** on a protein–protein interface (vs intrinsically disordered peptides); the pepkalc/Ising tools are built mostly for IDPs.

## Q2 — Concrete object modeled + metric/algorithm computable from MD / QM / diffusion outputs

### Takeaway
Every framework below reduces to an object you can build from existing pipeline outputs (a protonation microstate set, an interface point cloud, a contact graph, or two MD ensembles) and a scalar/curve you can optimize or filter on. Grouped by which are cheap post-processing vs heavy.

### Cited Findings
(Methods/objects are grounded in these sources; the project-specific operationalization is in Inferences.)
- **Ising/binding-polynomial object** = protonation microstate set {0,1}^N with intrinsic pKa vector + pairwise coupling matrix W_ij; algorithm = enumerate the 2^N partition function Z(pH), extract titration curve, n_H, capacitance dn̄/dpH, and dG_bind(pH) — quantitatively valid for a few coupled sites — [Coupled protonation / Ising](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2144168); [pepkalc](https://st-protein02.chem.au.dk/pepkalc/).
- **Wyman linkage object** = proton occupancy of bound vs free; algorithm = Δn_H(pH) curve → ΔΔG_pH integral, via constant-pH MD or a reference free energy — [Constant-pH MD + Wyman](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4306499).
- **3DZD object** = solvent-excluded surface patch; algorithm = 3D Zernike moment expansion → rotation-invariant coefficient vector (shape + ESP); complementarity = compare target vs binder vectors — [3DZD](https://bmcbioinformatics.biomedcentral.com/track/pdf/10.1186/1471-2105-10-407).
- **MaSIF object** = surface mesh with curvature + PB-electrostatics + H-bond features; algorithm = geodesic convolution → fingerprint; descriptor complementarity scores binding — [MaSIF](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/606202.full.pdf).
- **OT object** = two atom/point measures; algorithm = (fused supervised) Gromov–Wasserstein solve → invariant matching cost — [OTMol](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.01550).
- **MSM object** = discretized MD microstates; algorithm = transition matrix → implied timescales, MFPT, k_off; rare-event estimators for the tail — [Rare-event kinetics](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1409.6439).
- **TDA object** = atom point cloud / interface; algorithm = Vietoris–Rips filtration → persistence diagram; distance = bottleneck/Wasserstein between diagrams (novelty); ESPH features → affinity — [ESPH](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1703.10982); [PDFL](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.02596v1).
- **Spectral-graph object** = MD-weighted residue/interface contact graph; algorithm = Laplacian eigen-decomposition (Fiedler value = algebraic connectivity/rigidity), perturbation-response scanning, edge-betweenness — [Perturbation network analysis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6604606).
- **MSD object** = one sequence scored over a set of states; algorithm = minimax/Pareto over state energies (negative design) — [Rosetta MSF](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5484525).

### Inferences (concrete tests with the listed tools)
- **Cooperativity engine (a) — Ising from GFN2-xTB.** For a candidate 2–3-His (or His–Asp/Glu) cluster, enumerate the 2^N protonation microstates; for each, take the capped-cluster **GFN2-xTB** interaction energy (project memory: xTB is the fair, chemistry-unbiased choice vs fixed-charge MD) and/or **PROPKA** pairwise terms to populate W_ij and intrinsic pKas. Build Z(pH), read n_H and dG_bind(pH) over 6.0–7.4. Accept designs with n_H>1 and a large ∫Δn_H (Wyman). This directly screens RFd3/ProteinMPNN outputs for sharpness, and tells you the required inter-His spacing (RSC distance result).
- **Switch-sharpness gate (a) — bifurcation/Morse check.** From a 1-D pH-PMF or the dG_bind(pH) curve, test for a **saddle-node**: does pH-6 protonation *annihilate* the bound basin (not merely shallow it)? Build a 2-D PMF (or disconnectivity graph) from OpenMM MD in each protonation state; "basin removed at pH 6" = the catastrophe you want.
- **Flat-surface scoring (b).** Compute **3DZD (shape+ESP)** on 1TNF epitope sub-patches to rank the most-designable compact sub-site, and on each design's interface to score complementarity; as a differentiable alternative, compute a **Gromov–Wasserstein** cost between the binder's polar-group point cloud and the target's — both are post-hoc filters on RFd3/MPNN hits and cheap vs an AF2-ig/Boltz call. (MaSIF surface features are implementable even without the full model.)
- **Selectivity (c) — multistate oracle + kinetics.** Minimal MSD: run the binding oracle (**AF2-initial-guess / Boltz-2**) twice with pH-7.4 vs pH-6.0 protonation assignments and require PASS@7.4 ∧ FAIL@6.0; add **xTB ΔE_switch** on the capped cluster in both states. Heavy upgrade: **OpenMM MD → MSM (deeptime/PyEMMA)** in each state, compare k_off (MFPT) — a large k_off(6.0)/k_off(7.4) ratio is the kinetic selectivity signal.
- **Selectivity (c) — information-theoretic scalar.** From the two MD ensembles, compute the **Jensen–Shannon divergence** of interface contact-occupancy distributions; require it large *and localized* to binding contacts (a single cheap number from MD you already run; relative entropy is the fluctuation-response dual of linkage).
- **Novelty/diversity (d).** Compute persistence diagrams (ripser/GUDHI) per design; a bottleneck-distance matrix clusters seed lineages so the portfolio spreads (project memory "diverse, not cousins," cap ~2/lineage), complementing TM-score. Separately, penalize **persistent H2 voids at the interface** (packing defects that kill flat-surface binding).
- **Novelty frontier (d).** Plot binding-oracle score vs a novelty coordinate (1−TM, or TDA distance) across all designs and fit the empirical **rate-distortion frontier**; select points *on* the frontier — a principled formalization of the memory tension "Hill n>1 stacking fights novelty / safe-harbor TM<0.80."
- **Cooperative-collapse network test (a/c) — spectral graph.** Build the interface contact graph (MD-weighted), compute the Fiedler vector, and require the switch His to sit on a **high edge-betweenness / high-centrality node** whose protonation projects onto a *low-frequency global* interface mode (breaks many contacts at once = cooperative) rather than a local one.

### Gaps
- MSM-based k_off for a de-novo mini-binder is **sampling-limited**: the cited rare-event literature stresses every dissociation step must be sampled; whether the project's GPU budget supports converged k_off for both states is unverified.
- No benchmark found correlating **3DZD/GW interface-complementarity scores with AF2-ig/Boltz-2 PAE** specifically for flat cytokine epitopes, so these remain unvalidated filters until A/B-tested on project data.

## Q3 — Which are genuinely novel to protein design vs standard; highest-upside and riskiest

### Takeaway
Multistate/negative design, MSMs, spectral-network allostery, and 3DZD docking are **established** (low novelty, low risk, immediately usable). Persistent homology and optimal transport are **emerging** (medium novelty/risk). The **cooperative multi-site protonation switch via Ising/catastrophe/Wyman** is the highest-upside, genuinely-underused idea for *this* problem; rate-distortion, Morse-theoretic landscape design, and the speculative tail (tropical geometry, category theory) are the riskiest.

### Cited Findings
- **Standard / established (low risk):** multistate & negative design is mature in CPD (Rosetta MSF, ProteinInterfaceMSMover) — [MSF](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5484525); MSMs are "a key technology" for biomolecular kinetics — [Rare-event kinetics](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1409.6439); graph-spectral allostery has seen "a surge in recent years" and is a validated engineering tool — [Perturbation network analysis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6604606); 3DZD docking (LZerD) is a decade-plus established method — [3DZD](https://bmcbioinformatics.biomedcentral.com/track/pdf/10.1186/1471-2105-10-407).
- **Emerging (medium novelty/risk):** TDA/PH for binding is recent-and-growing, with 2024 extensions (persistent directed flag Laplacian) — [PDFL 2024](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.02596v1); OT for molecules is active (2025 fused-GW comparison) but mostly retrospective, **not yet generative conditioning** — [OTMol 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.01550); geometric-deep-learning surface design (MaSIF) is established-but-outside the project's listed stack — [MaSIF](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/606202.full.pdf).
- **Novel/high-upside for this problem:** the quantitative Ising/binding-polynomial treatment of coupled protonation exists in biophysics — [Ising protonation](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2144168) — and bifurcation↔Hill theory is well-developed — [Toggle bifurcations](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2509.24926) — but applying them as a *de-novo binder switch-design objective* is not something I found precedent for (see Gaps).

### Inferences
- **Highest upside, highest reward-to-effort: engineer n_H>1 via a coupled His cluster, scored by an xTB/PROPKA Ising model.** It attacks the project's main weakness (modest switch) at its mathematical root and is computable with tools already in hand. Risk: electrostatic frustration may hurt fold/novelty (memory: "stacking fights novelty"), and the achievable n_H on a rigid interface is unknown.
- **Lowest risk, do-now:** reframe the oracle stage as **two-state negative design** (PASS@7.4 ∧ FAIL@6.0) — essentially free given AF2-ig/Boltz are already run, and it directly fixes the "constitutive bridge passes direction but not selectivity" failure.
- **Best flat-surface bet:** add a **3DZD or Gromov–Wasserstein interface-complementarity filter** and a **persistent-H2 interface-void penalty** — cheap, pose-invariant, and complementary to AF2-ig/Boltz (which the memory shows can be fooled by self-sequence refolds).
- **Riskiest / most speculative (flag clearly):**
  - *Catastrophe/cusp design* — elegant target (annihilate the bound basin at pH 6) but no protein-interface precedent; treat as a diagnostic (basin-removal check) rather than a generator.
  - *Rate-distortion frontier* — a strong *organizing* principle for novelty-vs-binding but unlikely to yield a crisp algorithm beyond frontier selection.
  - *MSM k_off selectivity* — the most physically faithful selectivity readout but sampling-cost-prohibitive to converge; pursue only if a fast relaxation/rare-event estimator is adopted.
  - *Tropical geometry* — in principle the max-plus (low-T) limit of the protonation partition function makes the dominant microstate a tropical-polynomial max-term and the "switch region" a tropical hypersurface in (pH, design-parameter) space; **no protein-design precedent found** — pure speculation, not recommended to pursue now.
  - *Category theory* — no actionable metric for this problem surfaced; deprioritize.

### Gaps
- I found **no published instance of catastrophe/bifurcation theory or an Ising cooperativity objective being used to design a pH-switchable protein *binder*** specifically — strong indirect support (gene-switch bifurcations, IDP protonation Ising) but a genuine white space, so upside is inferred, not demonstrated.
- I found **no protein-design use of tropical geometry or category theory**; cannot confirm whether these are untried-because-useless or untried-because-novel.
- Could not source a head-to-head showing **OT/3DZD/TDA filters improve de-novo hit rates on flat polar cytokine epitopes**; novelty-vs-impact for the emerging methods is therefore a reasoned bet, not an evidenced one.
