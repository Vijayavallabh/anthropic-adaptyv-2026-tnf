# Exotic theory sharpens our TNF pH-switch

The single most actionable finding across the four notes is that the field's best precedent — Ahn et al. 2025, who designed TNF-alpha binders with **79-fold weaker binding at pH 5.4 vs 7.4** using histidine-biased ProteinMPNN plus a *single-structure* Rosetta `fa_elec` electrostatics delta, with **no PROPKA, no MD, no kinetics, and no cooperativity modeling** ([Ahn 2025, PMC12621740](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12621740/)) — provably lacks the two things our objective most rewards: a direct **kinetic (koff) score** for the pH-6.0 release and any explicit modeling of **cooperative sharpness** of the OFF transition. Every framework below is therefore "untried in the de novo binder loop" even where it is standard physics or decision theory elsewhere. The highest value-to-risk transfers all run in four days on *existing* pipeline outputs: a decision-theory selection stack (conformal prediction under feedback covariate shift, quality-weighted k-DPP batch selection, lexicographic priority enforcement, 1-D Wasserstein-barycenter oracle fusion) layered on cheap new physics/chemistry scores (Wyman-linkage Hill slope, APBS electrostatic sign-flip, Gromov-Wasserstein two-state distance). The two highest-ceiling-yet-feasible physics levers are koff scoring via infrequent metadynamics and cooperative-sharpness engineering via coupled histidine networks. The boldest genuinely-untried transfers — distributionally-robust max-min cross-reactivity, sheaf consistency-radius oracle fusion, and an acid-unfolding "conformational collapse" switch — have **no protein-design prior art at all**. And one decision gates the whole campaign and must be checked on day one: the pH-switch wants to exploit a *specific charged anchor* while cross-reactivity demands *substitution-robustness*, so if the switch-partner anchor is not conserved human→mouse, the two objectives collide.

## Ship these eight first: the four-day, low-risk tier

The lowest-risk, highest-leverage moves are post-processing layers on outputs the pipeline already produces — no generator retraining, all CPU-side, all `pip`-installable (`gudhi`, `persim`, `POT`, `dppy`, `mapie`, `scipy`). Ranked by value-to-risk:

| # | Transfer (field) | Concrete pipeline hook | 4-day risk | Untried vs emerging |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Conserved-anchor gate** (bioisostere / chemical robustness) | Align human (1TNF) vs mouse TNF, classify each groove-anchor contact as identical / conservative-swap / non-conservative, drive RFdiffusion hotspots + MPNN interface filter onto species-invariant contacts; score "fraction of interface energy from invariant contacts" | LOW (a day of PyRosetta + alignment) | Untried as explicit de-novo design rule |
| 2 | **Conformal selection under feedback covariate shift** + Learn-Then-Test | Turn each oracle score into a calibrated interval for bind@7.4 and bind@6.0; select only designs whose conformal set satisfies "yes@7.4 AND no@6.0"; LTT tunes the two thresholds to a joint error budget α | LOW–MEDIUM (needs a calibration anchor) | Untried in binder selection; one in-domain precedent |
| 3 | **Quality-weighted k-DPP / submodular** batch selection | k-DPP over the ~375 pool with kernel L = diag(q)·K·diag(q), q = reward, K = interface-embedding similarity; novelty gate applied as a hard filter *before* DPP | LOW (~30 lines numpy) | Untried as the <=20 mechanism |
| 4 | **Lexicographic / epsilon-constraint** priority | Filter by pH-selectivity threshold → then cross-reactivity → then rank by affinity; augmented-Chebyshev for final tie-break | LOW (20-line pandas sort) | Untried as pre-registered rule |
| 5 | **1-D Wasserstein-barycenter oracle fusion** | Fuse AF2-ig / Boltz-2 / Chai-1 per-design score distributions by averaging *quantile functions* (closed form in 1-D), reliability-weighted | LOW (closed form) | Untried for folding-oracle fusion |
| 6 | **Wyman-linkage Hill-slope + Ising cooperativity** score | From a PROPKA/pKAI/xtb pH ladder build Δn_H(pH) and ΔG_bind(pH), fit Hill n; select midpoint in [6.0,6.5] and max n; solve a 2–4-His Ising partition function (16 states) for collective sharpness | LOW (post-processing) | Untried as a selection filter |
| 7 | **APBS dG_elec sign-flip + charge-capacitance peak** | APBS nonlinear PB at pH-7.4 vs pH-6.0 protonation; require dG_elec(7.4)<0 (attractive) AND dG_elec(6.0)>0 (repulsive); reward interface capacitance C=−(1/ln10)dZ/dpH peaking near 6.3–6.6 | LOW (APBS in-stack) | Untried switch gate |
| 8 | **Gromov-Wasserstein / persistent-homology two-state** scores | GW distance between interface residue-distance matrices at pH 7.4 vs 6.0 (alignment-free "how much did geometry rearrange"); PH Wasserstein distance between the two protonation-state interface ensembles | LOW (`POT`, `gudhi`) | GW/PH emerging generally; two-state switch use untried |

The selection stack (rows 2–5) is where a few-shot campaign with noisy, partly-circular oracles is most likely won: **conformal prediction under feedback covariate shift is the one framework already validated on real protein design-selection data** and corrects the exact statistical pathology that our <=20 are chosen *by* the scores ([Fannjiang et al., PNAS 2022, arXiv:2202.03613](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.03613); [PMC9618043](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9618043)). Learn-Then-Test lets us control a *combined* "bind@7.4 AND not@6.0" risk without refitting ([Angelopoulos et al., arXiv:2110.01052](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2110.01052v4)), and conformal *selective* prediction formalizes "pick <=20 while controlling the false-positive rate" ([arXiv:2603.24704](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.24704)). Because the wet-lab screen is one-shot with no feedback round, the right acquisition is "maximize expected winners in the batch" (qPO / batched Thompson), not sequential information gain ([qPO, arXiv:2410.06333](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2410.06333); [batched Thompson, arXiv:2110.00202](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2110.00202)). Diversity is then enforced by a quality-weighted k-DPP, whose determinant jointly trades reward against repulsion of near-clones and which **"substantially improved diversity while still obtaining high-quality solutions" in de novo drug-design RL** ([arXiv:2506.21158](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.21158); DPP-Thompson tighter regret, [AISTATS 2022, arXiv:2110.11665](https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.11665)) — the exact operationalization of the campaign's "diverse, not cousins" rule. Crucially, linear weighted-sum scalarization **provably misses Pareto-optimal designs in non-convex regions**, so the strict priority is better served by lexicographic/epsilon-constraint filtering plus augmented-Tchebycheff tie-breaking ([non-convex caveat, arXiv:2210.12765](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2210.12765); [augmented Tchebycheff, arXiv:2305.11774](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2305.11774)).

### The switch-anchor / cross-reactivity collision — run this check first

The report's most decision-relevant finding is a tension the design cannot wish away. **A sharp pH-switch is built by exploiting a specific charged anchor** — e.g., clustering interface histidines against a conserved basic TNF residue such as K65 so protonation at pH 6.0 turns the patch repulsive (the chemistry route in rows 6–7). **Cross-reactivity, by contrast, demands robustness to the 21% of positions that differ between human and mouse TNF** (79% identity), which argues for contacting the backbone or conservative-swap-tolerant chemistry rather than any one side chain ([conservative-substitution groups, US Patent 6783936](https://image-ppubs.uspto.gov/dirsearch-public/print/downloadPdf/6783936); [cross-reactive epitope substitution sensitivity, US Patent 11447566](https://image-ppubs.uspto.gov/dirsearch-public/print/downloadPdf/11447566)). If the switch-partner anchor is itself a non-conserved residue, the two top objectives directly collide and the switch must be rebuilt on a conserved anchor. This is a go/no-go, not a tuning knob: the conservation status of K65/Q67/E135/E146 **and** the chosen switch partner must be confirmed from UniProt / 1TNF vs mouse TNF *before any switch chemistry is committed*. The resolution is cheap and principled — anchor specific switch contacts only on conserved positions, make contacts to conservative-swap positions geometrically tolerant (a carboxylate bidentate satisfies both Asp and Glu; a cation-π contact tolerates Lys and Arg), and contact the backbone wherever a non-conservative swap lands. Distributionally-robust selection (next section) then formally rewards exactly the binders whose score is *flat* across the human→mouse ambiguity, giving the tension a number to rank on.

## Kinetics and cooperativity are the two levers the baseline lacks

These two physics frameworks are the highest-ceiling transfers that are still four-day-feasible, and both are *absent* from the Ahn baseline — making them the natural headline novelties for the dossier.

**Release is fundamentally a kinetic event, so score koff directly.** The objective "release at pH 6.0" is a dissociation-rate statement, yet no prior de-novo loop scores it. The cheapest usable method is **infrequent (frequency-adaptive) metadynamics**, which recovers an *unbiased* koff from mean residence time and reproduces brute-force MD's profile, pathway, and residence time at **6–50× lower cost** ([PMC5451192](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5451192); [frequency-adaptive, arXiv:1802.04182](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/1802.04182)). Run it once per protonation state in OpenMM/PLUMED, biasing a binder–TNF separation CV, and select on the ratio koff(6.0)/koff(7.4); for the top 2–3 designs, **weighted ensemble (WESTPA)** gives koff with confidence intervals and the unbinding pathway, telling us *which* His-mediated contact ruptures first ([PMC8825686](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8825686)). A **Kramers** prefactor on a cheap umbrella/SMD PMF gives a relative koff for fast triage, since protein barrier crossing sits in the diffusive limit where koff ∝ (1/γ)·exp(−ΔG‡/kT) ([RSC c8sc01319e](https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlehtml/2018/sc/c8sc01319e)) — notably, a protonated, more rigid interface may *raise friction γ at pH 6.0*, a second koff lever beyond barrier height. As an absolute-ΔG reference, **Jarzynski/Crooks SMD** pulls (bidirectional, with a BAR estimate) ranked affinities at experimental correlation **R = 0.80–0.89** across six targets ([PMC7590978](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7590978)). The shared risk is honest: convergence on a *protein–protein* interface within four days is uncertain, so every kinetic number is a *relative* (7.4-vs-6.0, design-vs-design) ranking filter, never an absolute s⁻¹. The design target is clean: maximize ΔG‡(7.4) − ΔG‡(6.0) so pH 7.4 kinetically traps the binder and pH 6.0 presents a low, broad escape barrier.

**A sharp OFF requires cooperative titration — a single His is too shallow.** One histidine gives a Henderson-Hasselbalch curve spanning ~2 pH units from 10%→90%; our target window is ~0.4 units. Wyman-Tanford linkage makes this rigorous: the steepness d(ΔG_bind)/d(pH) = −2.303·RT·Δn_protons, so sharpness *requires* multiple titratable sites that flip protonation state on binding ([da Rocha et al., JCTC 2022, PMC9775224](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9775224); [Wyman linkage synthesis, Stony Brook](https://researchconnect.stonybrook.edu/en/publications/analysis-of-the-ph-dependent-folding-and-stability-of-histidine-p/)). The proven protein-encodable route is the Baker/Boyken buried **histidine hydrogen-bond network**: transition *steepness scales with the number of coupled His*, the single-His variant was "the least cooperative design tested," and protonating one His disrupts the network to make the rest more accessible — textbook positive cooperativity ([Science 364 aav7897, 2019](https://www.ipd.uw.edu/publication-pdfs/4/517f778cf57bbe00f3bcd20be228243a/science.aav7897.pdf); [PMC7072037](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7072037)). The beyond-Baker transfer — genuinely untried — is to move that cooperative network **from the fold core to the binder–TNF interface**: a 3-His network that straddles the groove is a neutral H-bond zipper holding the complex at pH 7.4, and at pH 6.0 collective protonation injects +2/+3 into a buried, low-dielectric pocket, paying a large desolvation penalty plus mutual repulsion that breaks the interface. This fuses the sharpness lever (rows 6) with the desolvation lever: select interface His that are *buried in the complex* but *solvent-exposed in the free binder*, so the free binder never pays the penalty and stays folded. Coupling can be modeled immediately — a 2–4-site Ising/Potts partition function (16 microstates, ~50 lines) with Coulomb couplings J_ij predicts the collective midpoint and cooperativity sign, and an MWC two-state conformational amplification (now demonstrated de novo with cooperativity "comparable to haemoglobin") is the sharpest achievable mechanism if a hinge is buildable ([de novo MWC, Nature 2024 / bioRxiv 2023.11.01.565167](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2023.11.01.565167.full.pdf)). The sign caveat is real: His-His repulsion often gives *negative* cooperativity that broadens the curve, so placement geometry decides sign and must be checked. That histidine is the right sensor is independently confirmed by pH-dependent antibody engineering, which uses His (pKa ~6.0) precisely to accelerate dissociation below pH 6.5 for endosomal release — our exact direction ([PMC4622719](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4622719)).

The unifying insight that makes both levers cheap: **one short constant-pH (or two-fixed-state) OpenMM ensemble per design simultaneously yields the Wyman ΔG(pH) and per-residue drivers, the variance-based Hill sharpness, and the LRA cross-reactivity sensitivities**, because d(n)/d(pH) = −2.303·var(n) and the per-site linkage is −2.303·cov(nᵢ,n) — the cooperativity, selectivity, and mutation-sensitivity scores are mathematically the *same* fluctuation object computed from one trajectory ([PMC9775224](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9775224)). A **GFN2-xTB microstate pKa** (SAMPL6 MAE 1.74 / RMSE 2.18 pKa units) adds a burial- and coupling-aware independent leg beyond PROPKA/pKAI ([k-dense SAMPL6](https://www.k-dense.ai/use-cases/sampl6-first-principles-pka-prediction); [RSC d0cp05281g](https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlehtml/2021/cp/d0cp05281g)), though its precision is marginal for resolving a midpoint to ±0.3 units and so it serves as a consensus vote, not a sole oracle.

## Higher-ceiling bets worth a GPU gamble

Beyond the feasible tier sit bolder transfers — some with no protein-design prior art whatsoever — that could become the campaign's differentiated contribution if the low-risk stack converges early.

| Bet (field) | Why it could be decisive | Risk / posture |
|---|---|---|
| **Wasserstein-DRO for cross-reactivity** (robust optimization) | Cross-reactivity *is* a distribution-shift problem; rank by worst-case (min) predicted affinity over an ambiguity set {human, mouse, perturbed targets}. Formally justifies "design on conserved anchors" and gives it a number | LOW cost as a *selection* min; genuinely untried in protein design |
| **Sheaf consistency-radius oracle/multi-chain fusion** (applied topology) | A principled "do my oracles/chains agree" scalar replacing ad-hoc "require all three to pass"; flags designs that only work on one protomer of the trimer | MEDIUM (`pysheaf` finicky; hand-roll ~30–50 lines); untried in protein design |
| **Acid-unfolding "conformational collapse" switch** (polymer physics) | A *global* buried-His core-destabilizer that unfolds at pH 6.0 removes the whole interface at once — intrinsically sharp (near-first-order) and fast koff | HIGH design risk (marginal stability → aggregation); bold secondary track |
| **GFlowNet reward-proportional generation** (ML) | Samples in proportion to a multi-objective reward, not argmax → diverse novel portfolio; a pH-switch-conditioned reward is genuinely untried | LOW at sequence/pool level, HIGH at backbone level |
| **Morse / HiSD transition-barrier scoring** (dynamical systems) | The index-1 saddle on the bound→disassembled path *is* the switch barrier — a kinetic complement to equilibrium ΔG_pH | MEDIUM (NEB/HiSD in OpenMM is the heaviest item) |
| **VSE / preorganized electric-field pKa tuning** (physical chemistry) | The field projected onto the switch His imidazole is a single-number, physically-grounded pKa dial; a helix macrodipole shifts pKa ~1 unit | LOW compute, MEDIUM calibration; mechanistic layer |
| **Causal do-calculus mechanism test** (decision theory) | Formalizes isostere ablation as do(His→Gln, matched sterics) to prove the switch is *caused* by titratability, not a constitutive salt bridge | LOW compute, high rigor |

The three with no protein-design precedent at all are the headline candidates. **Distributionally-robust optimization** treats the human/mouse pair as a Wasserstein ambiguity ball and selects binders by their worst-case score over {human-TNF, mouse-TNF, plus in-silico perturbations at non-conserved positions}; this needs no DRO solver — the min over a sampled target set *is* the empirical worst-case risk — yet no source applies DRO to binder design at all, which is itself the signal ([Wasserstein-DRO theory, arXiv:2105.08414](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.08414); [arXiv:1805.06729](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1805.06729)). **Sheaf theory** is the canonical mathematics for fusing disagreeing local observations into a global-consistency score; the consistency radius becomes a per-design trust score (oracles agree → submit; disagree → demote) and a multi-chain consistency check across the trimer's symmetric interfaces ([Robinson, arXiv:1603.01446](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1603.01446)), directly operationalizing the campaign lesson that a single co-folder's cross-species delta is folder pessimism. The **acid-unfolding switch** borrows the coil-globule transition — which can be discontinuous (first-order-like, i.e. sharp) — to make the binder tip into a non-binding molten globule at pH 6.0 ([coil-globule theory, arXiv:cond-mat/0612256](https://arxiv.org/pdf/cond-mat/0612256)); it is the sharpest and fastest mechanism in principle but the riskiest to build, and should use a **buried His** (natural in-window pKa), never a buried acid, since buried-acid pKa is unpredictable by 2–3 units. GFlowNets round out the bold tier: the reward-proportional sampler is established for sequences ([Jain et al., ICML 2022, arXiv:2203.04115](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.04115)) and emerging for antibody binding ([AbFlowNet, arXiv:2505.12358](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.12358)), but a pH-conditioned reward on de-novo mini-binders is frontier — do it at the sequence step (soluble-MPNN-logit-initialized) or, lazier, as reward-proportional *resampling* of the existing ~375 pool, never backbone-level in four days. The remaining imports (1-bit channel-capacity switch discriminability, conceptual-DFT Fukui/HSAB partner selection, GVP/Clifford equivariant interface featurizers at a modest ~2% gain, and topological-protection normal-mode robustness) are secondary ranking or framing layers, not core bets.

## An integrated four-day pipeline

The transfers compose into one coherent campaign that reuses every existing GPU output and adds only CPU-side scoring plus a focused MD shortlist. **Day zero is the gate:** run the human-vs-mouse conservation map, classify every groove-anchor contact, and confirm the switch-partner anchor is conserved — this resolves the switch/cross-reactivity collision before compute is spent, and it drives RFdiffusion hotspot choice and the MPNN interface filter toward species-invariant contacts and interface-straddling cooperative 3-His networks. **Generation** stays RFdiffusion + soluble-ProteinMPNN, optionally augmented by a sequence-level GFlowNet or pool-resampling with a priority-weighted reward. **Cheap per-design scoring** (pure post-processing of existing structures) adds the APBS dG_elec sign-flip and capacitance peak, the GFN2-xTB microstate pKa leg, the Wyman Hill-slope from the pKa panel, the GW and persistent-homology two-state distances, the VSE field projection, and a Fukui/HSAB partner tie-break. **One short constant-pH OpenMM ensemble per shortlisted design** then yields, from a single trajectory, the linkage ΔG(pH), the variance-based Hill sharpness, the per-residue drivers, and the LRA cross-reactivity sensitivities. **The top few go to infrequent metadynamics** for koff(6.0)/koff(7.4), with WESTPA confirmation on the final two or three. **Fusion and selection** close the loop: reliability-weighted 1-D Wasserstein-barycenter oracle fusion, conformal + LTT calibration of the joint "bind@7.4 AND not@6.0" criterion, a worst-case DRO score over {human, mouse, perturbations}, a sheaf consistency-radius trust filter, lexicographic filtering (selectivity → cross-reactivity → affinity), and finally a quality-weighted k-DPP that picks the <=20 with quality = conformal lower-confidence-bound on selectivity and kernel = interface-embedding similarity. Portfolio coverage is reported as the kernel log-determinant plus a persistent-homology cluster count to guard against submitting twenty cousins of one seed, and the final set's switch mechanism is confirmed by a matched-sterics His→Gln do-intervention that distinguishes a real titratable switch from a constitutive salt bridge.

## Conclusion

The deepest takeaway reframes the whole effort: **cooperative sharpness, pH selectivity, and cross-reactivity are not three separate scores but one fluctuation object** — the variance and covariance of proton number in a single constant-pH ensemble — so the "expensive" rigorous engine is actually one simulation per design that the Ahn baseline simply never ran. The second reframe is that in a few-shot, novelty-gated, one-shot-screen campaign with noisy and partly-circular oracles, the decisive gains likely come not from a better generator but from the **decision-theory layer**: conformal risk control makes the "bind-and-release" guarantee quantitative, DRO turns cross-reactivity into a worst-case number that formally rewards conserved-anchor design, and quality-weighted k-DPP converts "diverse not cousins" from a slogan into a determinant. The two bolder physics bets — kinetic koff scoring and interface-straddling cooperative His networks — are where the ceiling is highest and the prior art is emptiest. But the irreducible risk is biological, not computational: whether a sharp charged-anchor switch and genuine mouse cross-reactivity can coexist is decided by *where the conserved epitope actually sits*, which is why the conservation-map gate, not any exotic method, is the first thing to run and the one result that can redirect the entire campaign.
